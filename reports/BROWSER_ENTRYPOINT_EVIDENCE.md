## What would upgrade a candidate

A useful 14.10 record must retain the console version, exact menu/link sequence, prerequisites, final page origin, actual execution device, diagnostic revision/hash and full completion summary. Photographs or video should distinguish the TV from a companion phone and omit account names, QR authentication tokens, network credentials and device identifiers. A server GET is evidence of a request only; browser/app previews or another device can request files too. A JavaScript summary visible on the identified console is stronger.

No published source inspected supplies that complete record. This is a bounded negative finding, not proof that all undiscovered routes are impossible. Historical posts without a firmware identity cannot be promoted based on publication recency or a title saying “works every time.” No firmware binary, exploit, DNS service, portal interception or console experiment was used.
